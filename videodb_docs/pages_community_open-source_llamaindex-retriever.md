## LlamaIndex Documentation

View the official LlamaIndex VideoDB Retriever documentation

Today, most of the RAG applications are limited to generating text responses. However, VideoDB's integration with the popular RAG framework, LlamaIndex, introduces a groundbreaking dimension to RAG, upon video repositories. This integration enables RAG to produce multimodal outputs, including video streams alongside text results, enhancing the verifiability of these results. It also opens up numerous practical applications where viewing the stream is essential, such as in security camera footage, OTT content, and news and media.
