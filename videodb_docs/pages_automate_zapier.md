# Zapier Workflows

Copy page

Zapier is a no-code automation platform that connects VideoDB with thousands of apps. Build automated workflows to process videos, generate content, and sync data.

Copy page

## [ Available Workflows](#available-workflows)

## YouTube Summaries to Notion

Summarize new YouTube videos and save to Notion

## Video Dubbing to Drive

Dub videos into Spanish and store in Google Drive

## GenAI Video to YouTube

Generate AI videos from Notion ideas and publish to YouTube

## Profanity Detection

Detect profanity in videos and send Slack alerts

## Subtitle Generation

Generate subtitles and save videos to Google Drive

## Video Highlights

Extract highlights and summaries to Notion

## Video Q&amp;A Support

Answer Zendesk tickets using video content

## [ Getting Started](#getting-started)

To use VideoDB with Zapier, you need:

1. A Zapier account
2. A VideoDB API key from the [VideoDB Console](https://console.videodb.io/auth?utm_source=docs_videodb_io&utm_medium=docs_link&utm_campaign=console_auth&utm_content=docs_link&id=docs)
3. Connected accounts for the apps you want to integrate (Google Drive, Notion, Slack, etc.)

Each workflow guide includes prerequisites, step-by-step instructions, and video tutorials.
