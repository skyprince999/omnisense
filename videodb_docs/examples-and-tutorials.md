## [ What Do You Want to Build?](#what-do-you-want-to-build)

## Agentic Workflows

**Plug video into your agents.** Give your AI context from meetings, recordings, desktop, and cameras. Build pair programmers, agent monitoring, and personal work memory.

## Video Search and Understanding

**Find and understand videos.** Turn hours of video into searchable knowledge. Query meetings, lectures, or archives with natural language and get timestamped, playable results.

## Live Intelligence

**Monitor cameras &amp; streams.** Real-time AI detection for security, safety, traffic, and sports. Get instant alerts when events happen.

## Content Factory

**Create videos at scale.** Automate production with AI dubbing, voiceovers, faceless videos, and text-to-video generation. No manual editing required.

## Programmatic Editing

**Edit video with code.** Add intros, overlays, and dynamic elements using the Timeline API. Generate multiple versions from one source.

## Safety &amp; Compliance

**Moderate &amp; protect.** Detect profanity, filter inappropriate content, and identify copyright violations automatically.
