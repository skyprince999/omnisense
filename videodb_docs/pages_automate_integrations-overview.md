## [ Automation Platforms](#automation-platforms)

VideoDB integrates with leading automation platforms, allowing you to build powerful no-code video workflows without writing any code.

### [ n8n](#n8n)

**n8n** is an open-source, self-hosted workflow automation platform that gives you complete control over your video processing pipelines.

- **Self-hosted or cloud** - Deploy on your own infrastructure or use n8n Cloud
- **Visual workflow builder** - Create complex workflows with an intuitive drag-and-drop interface
- **Extensive app ecosystem** - Connect to 400+ apps and services
- **Enterprise-ready** - Advanced features like error handling, scheduling, and monitoring

Perfect for teams that want full control over their automation infrastructure and need to process videos at scale.

### [ Zapier](#zapier)

**Zapier** is a cloud-based automation platform that connects thousands of apps without code.

- **No infrastructure needed** - Everything runs in the cloud, nothing to set up or maintain
- **Millions of users** - Trusted by teams worldwide for no-code automation
- **App marketplace** - Connect to 6000+ apps including Google Drive, Slack, Notion, and more
- **Instant setup** - Get workflows running in minutes with pre-built templates

Ideal for teams that want to quickly automate video workflows across their favorite tools without managing any infrastructure.

## [ Which Platform Should I Choose?](#which-platform-should-i-choose)

| Feature              | n8n                                           | Zapier                                   |
|----------------------|-----------------------------------------------|------------------------------------------|
| **Hosting**          | Self-hosted or cloud                          | Cloud-only                               |
| **Setup Complexity** | Simple (cloud) or moderate (self-hosted)      | Very simple (instant)                    |
| **Cost Structure**   | Pay for infrastructure                        | Per-task pricing                         |
| **Control**          | Full customization                            | Limited customization                    |
| **Best For**         | Teams needing full control, complex workflows | Quick automation, pre-built integrations |

Both platforms offer powerful video automation capabilities. Choose **n8n** if you need full control and self-hosting, or choose **Zapier** if you want the fastest way to get started with minimal setup.
